About Us

Groundwork you don't see, engineered so it never lets you down

Sun Pile Foundation specialises in advanced piling and foundation engineering — built on quality, safety and timely execution.

Our story

A foundation contractor built around one discipline

Sun Pile Foundation was formed to do one thing well: get the piling and foundation stage of a project right, every time. Our commitment to quality, safety, and timely execution has helped us deliver reliable foundation systems for residential, commercial, and industrial developments across the region.

We work directly with structural engineers, contractors and property owners — reading soil reports, matching the right piling method to the ground, and staying on site until every pile is tested and signed off.

What guides us

Three things every project is built on

01

Quality

Every pile is installed and tested to specification — nothing is signed off on assumption.

02

Safety

Site crews follow strict safety practice around heavy plant, deep bores and reinforcement work.

03

Timely execution

We plan rig mobilisation and pours around your project schedule, not the other way round.

Who we work with

From single plots to large industrial sites

Our teams have delivered foundation systems for residential builders, commercial developers, and industrial and infrastructure contractors. The scale changes; the process — soil assessment, method selection, installation, and load testing — stays the same.
