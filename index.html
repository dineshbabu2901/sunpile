About Sun Pile

Foundation work is the part no one sees — and the part everything depends on

We specialise in piling and foundation engineering: reading the soil, choosing the right method, and installing piles that hold their load for the life of the structure. From single-plot buildings to large industrial sites, we run the same disciplined process every time.

What we do

Piling methods matched to your soil and structure

Four proven techniques, each suited to a different ground condition and load requirement.

S 01

Cast-in-Situ Rotary Hydraulic Pile

Piles bored and poured directly on site using rotary hydraulic rigs — a strong, durable foundation for heavier structural loads.

S 02

Bored Direct Mud Circulation Pile

Mud-circulation drilling keeps the bore stable in loose or water-bearing soil, so the pile shaft holds its shape while pouring.

S 03

Under-Reamed Pile

A widened base bulb spreads the load over a larger bearing area — well suited to expansive, black cotton soils.

S 04

Load Test on Pile

On-site testing confirms a pile carries its designed load safely before the structure above it is built.
